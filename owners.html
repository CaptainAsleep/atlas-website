The Honest Comparison

We don't know your current setup — SmartWaiver, Eventbrite, a clipboard. Here's the honest case either way.

Save on waivers. Standalone tools like SmartWaiver charge separately for this, every month. Waivers are already built into Atlas.
Save on payment processing. No separate payment processor to set up or maintain. Atlas runs it through Stripe for you — and the booking fee is paid by the player, not you.
Save on event bookings. Generic ticketing tools like Eventbrite or TicketSpice charge their own booking fee on top of card processing — often $1–3 extra per ticket. Event creation is already part of Atlas.
Save on time. QR check-in means no clipboard at the gate and no cross-referencing who's actually paid. Scan, confirmed, next.
