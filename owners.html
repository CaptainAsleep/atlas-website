What switching actually saves you

We don't know your exact current setup — maybe it's SmartWaiver, GoDaddy Payments, Square, a paper clipboard at the gate. Here's the honest case either way.

Stop paying separately for waivers. Standalone waiver tools like SmartWaiver run $19–55+/month on their own, plus a separate add-on just to keep old signatures on file. Atlas includes real, reusable, e-signed waivers as part of the platform — not a bolt-on subscription you're paying for twice.
Stop juggling three logins for one event. A waiver tool, a booking calendar, and a payment processor that don't talk to each other means re-entering the same information three times. Atlas replaces the juggling, not just adds another tab.
We're honest about payment processing. Standard card processing (roughly 2.9% + $0.30) is the same everywhere — Stripe, Square, or anyone else — and Atlas doesn't pretend to beat that. What's actually different: our player booking fee is paid by the player, on top of your listed price. It's never skimmed off your own revenue.
If you're on GoDaddy Payments today — worth knowing it has no way to support real event bookings or anything resembling a marketplace at all. It's built for simple storefront checkout, not what actually running a field's events requires.
