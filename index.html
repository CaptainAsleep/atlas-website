Claim your field

Airsoft has never really had infrastructure.

Fields run game day on paper waivers, a clipboard at the gate, and a Venmo screenshot for payment. Players sign the same liability waiver over and over, show up not knowing if a field is even open that day, and walk away with no record of the games they've played.

Atlas is the operational layer underneath all of that: waivers players e-sign once, event booking with real live capacity, QR check-in at the gate instead of a clipboard, and payouts that land with the field automatically.

Everything underneath is real. Hours, rules, and events are kept current by the field owners themselves. No filler, no invented stats.

Started in Michigan, built to grow field by field, state by state.
