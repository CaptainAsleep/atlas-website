About Atlas

Atlas started with a simple problem: finding a real airsoft or paintball field, with real hours and real event info, shouldn't mean digging through outdated directories and half-abandoned Facebook groups.

So Atlas is built on real data — fields and events with actual hours, rules, and amenities, kept current by the field owners themselves wherever possible. No filler, no invented stats.

Started in Michigan, built to grow field by field, state by state.
