Frequently asked questions

What counts toward my events-per-month limit?
Published events on your field within a calendar month. Drafts you haven't published yet don't count.
What happens if I go over my plan's limit?
You'll be prompted to upgrade before you can publish beyond your plan's event or capacity limit — nothing gets blocked without warning.
Is there a fee when players book my events?
Yes — a flat $2 per booking, paid by the player on top of your entry cost. It's separate from your monthly plan and never reduces what you receive.
When does billing actually start?
October 24, 2026. You can claim your field and get everything set up before then at no cost.
