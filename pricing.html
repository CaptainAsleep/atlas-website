Every plan includes

Full field profile
Hours, amenities, rules, chrono limits, rental gear, and a photo gallery — all in your control.
Drafts, publishing, and duplication
Build an event as a draft, publish when it's ready, and duplicate past events instead of starting from scratch.
Real analytics
Published events, total interest, bookings, and waiver signatures — honest numbers about your own field, not vanity stats.
Competing-events heads-up
A warning if another nearby field has a big event the same day, so you can plan around it.
Check-in reward patches
Attach a patch to an event and it's granted automatically the moment a player checks in.
Player bans
Ban a problem player from your field directly, right from your roster.
Direct support
Reach the team directly in our Discord — real answers, not a ticket queue.
Player booking fee: when a player books a spot at your event, Atlas adds a small booking fee on top of your event's entry cost — 10% of the price, capped at $3 no matter how expensive the event. That fee is paid by the player, not deducted from what you charge — it never comes out of your revenue.
