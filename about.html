About Atlas

Airsoft has never really had infrastructure. Fields run game day on paper waivers, a clipboard at the gate, and a Venmo screenshot for payment. Players sign the same liability waiver over and over, show up not knowing if a field is even open that day, and walk away with no record of the games they've played.

Atlas is the operational layer underneath all of that — waivers players e-sign once, event booking with real live capacity, QR check-in at the gate instead of a clipboard, and payouts that land with the field automatically. Helping players find a field and an event is part of it, but it's the starting point, not the whole platform.

Everything underneath is real — hours, rules, and events kept current by the field owners themselves. No filler, no invented stats.

Started in Michigan, built to grow field by field, state by state.
